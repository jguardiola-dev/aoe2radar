Everything you need for AoE2. And more than you imagined.

A free Windows app that follows your friends and the pros, shows you who your opponent really is when the game starts, and puts your build order on top of the game. Without spoilers.

Built for the moment you play

What you would otherwise piece together from five browser tabs, or simply couldn't do, in one window next to the game.

Build orders that follow your game

38 build orders from Hera, TaToH, Sebastian, Survivalist and more, step by step with icons. Start one in game and it sits on top of AoE2, moving to the next step on its own with the game clock.

  • When the game ends, your times are compared with the build's.
  • Search by name, author or civ, filter by difficulty and keep your favourites.

Your opponent, the moment the game starts

When your game begins, aoe2radar shows your opponent's real 1v1 and team ELO, whether the account is someone's smurf (and whose), and the civs of their last five 1v1 games.

  • Know if they always pick the same civ before the first villager is out.

A Watchlist that tells you when they play

Follow your friends, your clan and the pros, in groups. As soon as one of them starts a game you see who they are playing against, on which map, with which civs and at what ELO, and you can spectate it in one click.

  • Turn on the bell for a player and get an alert even while you are in game.
  • It stays docked on the left in every tab, with each player's recent form.

Anyone's profile, down to their speed

Peak ELO for every mode, the rating chart, favourite civs and maps, head-to-head against you, and their real speed in effective actions per minute (eAPM).

  • See their previous nicks and linked accounts, copy their ID or open them on aoe2insights.

Spoiler-free recs, straight into the game

Find any player's games and send the rec to AoE2 in one click: it shows up in Saved games, ready to watch. The result stays hidden until you click the eye.

  • Search by player, ELO and map, or play «Guess the ELO» with random games.

Balanced teams and your own league

Add 2 to 8 players and aoe2radar splits them into teams by ELO, with a map wheel and fair civs. Then start a league with friends: one code to join, and the results count themselves from the games you play.

  • Copy the lobby name, find the league's games and see civ win rates for each map.

Settle the fight before you make the units

Set up a battle with exactly the upgrades you want, see who wins and why, and then send it to the game as a scenario to watch it happen. The villager calculator tells you how many villagers each resource needs to keep production going non-stop.

  • In AoE2: Editor, Load scenario, Test, and the game says «P1 wins» or «P2 wins».

Every tournament, with all the details

Liquipedia tournaments and gamenex.gg community cups in one place: dates, prize pool, brackets, results, the maps and civs of every game, and the next match of the players you follow.

  • Pin a tournament to Home and never miss a match.

And everything else you use every day

    The best games being played right now, with ELO and civs, ready to spectate.

  • All the stats

    Every ladder by mode, country and clan, ELO charts, and the win rate of every civ by map and ELO.

Also: a Home you arrange your way, light and dark themes, English and Spanish, and a match viewer in beta.

Spoiler-free by design

aoe2radar is made for watching games without knowing how they end. Nothing gives the result away unless you ask for it.

  • No winner in the list. Game lists never show who won, the ELO change or the game length.
  • ELO that doesn't tell. Watchlist ELO only refreshes when you open the app, never when you search.
  • You decide. The eye on each game reveals a crown or a skull, only when you click it.

It keeps itself up to date

  • It updates itself. New versions download in the background and apply when you close the app. If a start ever fails, it goes back to the previous version on its own.
  • Nightly data. Profiles, ladders and civ stats are prepared every night, so the app asks the live services for as little as possible.
  • Current patch. The tech tree follows every new patch.

Install it in three steps

  1. Download aoe2radar-1.5-setup.exe.
  2. Run it. It installs for your Windows user only, without administrator rights, and adds a Start menu entry.
  3. If SmartScreen warns with Windows protected your PC, click More info and then Run anyway. The installer is not code-signed yet, which is why Windows warns.

Rather not install anything? Every version also has a portable zip, in all versions.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no account and no paid version.

Does it touch the game or my account?

No. It doesn't modify Age of Empires II and never asks for your login. It reads public match data, downloads recs and, only when you ask, opens a game or a scenario in AoE2.

What does it send about me?

Nothing. No analytics, no telemetry. Your settings and lists stay on your PC.

Is there a Mac or Linux version?

Not for now: aoe2radar is a Windows app, like the game.

Can I change the language or the theme?

Yes. It speaks English and Spanish, has light and dark themes and three font sizes.

I found a bug or I have an idea. Where do I tell you?

In the app, from the ⚙ menu, or in GitHub issues. You can also find me on Twitch.
